ASD-STE100 at a glance

Summary

The current edition (January 2025) has 53 writing rules and a dictionary of approximately 900 approved words. Keep sentences short, use the active voice, and write one instruction per sentence.

How STE developed
1983
After an investigation into controlled languages, AECMA decided to produce its own controlled English.
1985
The AECMA Simplified English Guide (document PSC-85-16598) was released.
1986
Since 1986, STE has been a requirement of ATA Specification i2200.
2004
AECMA merged with two other associations into ASD. The document was renamed ASD Simplified Technical English, Specification ASD-STE100.
Jan 2025
Current edition published: 53 writing rules and approximately 900 approved words.
Events in date order; spacing between events is not to scale.
